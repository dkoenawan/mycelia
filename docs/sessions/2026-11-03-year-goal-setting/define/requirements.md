# Requirements: Year goal setting: primary goal as a Mandala Chart, plus secondary goals

> Part of [index](index.md) · Define

## Requirements

EARS syntax. Each requirement has one acceptance criterion in Given/When/Then form. IDs are never reused; a dropped requirement is struck through, not deleted. A Won't meaning "never" is struck through with its reason; a Won't meaning "later" moves to the Deferred table below.

- **"Year-goals note"** means the one Markdown note that holds a year's goals: its main goals, its secondary goals, a status (`draft` or `signed-off`) and a changes section. Each year has one such note.
- **"Main goal"** means a goal drafted as a Mandala Chart: a **centre goal** statement, the **pillars** that serve it (the Mandala Chart has up to 8), and the **actions** under each pillar (up to 8). A note may hold several main goals. More than 3 is strongly discouraged but allowed. A partly filled chart is valid. The issue's "primary goal" is a main goal.
- **"Secondary goal"** means a one-line goal statement with no chart, plus zero or more areas it touches. A goal that is worth charting is a main goal; anything lighter is a secondary goal.
- **"Areas touched"** means `[[wikilinks]]` to `20-areas/` notes, recorded on a pillar or a secondary goal. They mark where relevant knowledge accumulates. They put nothing into the area notes.
- **"Validator"** and **"parser"** mean the framework's check and reader for the year-goals note format. Each runs on one note path. The validator reports **errors**, which reject the note and are kept for content that makes the note unreadable as a year-goals note, and **warnings**, which never reject it and are used for every rule about how the operator structures their goals.
- **"Goal-setting session"** means the agent-guided conversation in which the operator and the agent draft a year-goals note.
- **"Current goals for a year"** means that year's year-goals note in `01-vision/` with status `signed-off`.
